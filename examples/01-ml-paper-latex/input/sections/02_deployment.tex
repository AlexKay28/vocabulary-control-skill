\section{Deployment}

The deployment landscape is a dazzling tapestry of trade-offs, and the
diffusion model, the DG arm, the diffusion model with blocks, and the
block-diffusion variant, and the diffusion arm all serve as a testament to the versatility of the
approach, while the baseline, a metered taxi whose fare climbs with every
token before you can blink, delves into the intricacies of sequential
decoding, unlocking the true potential of a fast-paced, cutting-edge,
game-changing pipeline that redefines the paradigm shift of log processing
in the ever-evolving world of production clusters.

Latency is a knob that teams turn when budgets tighten, and it is important
to note that the robust and scalable nature of the design paves the way for
a seamless transition from prototype to production.
