\section{Introduction}

Log parsing pipelines turn raw cluster logs into structured records, and the
parse must stay fast enough to keep up with ingestion. We study a diffusion
model against an autoregressive baseline on this task, holding model size,
data, and evaluation fixed so the comparison isolates the decoding scheme.
The diffusion model reaches a median latency of 23 ms per event, while the
baseline takes 47 ms under the same budget, and both fill every required
field on the same held-out split.

The claim is narrow. Under matched quality, parallel field filling removes
the sequential-token latency of the baseline, and the measured gap holds
across three seeds and two input lengths. Section 2 states the cost model,
and Section 3 reports the measured deployment behavior.
