# Paper C: speculative decoding with a draft model (synthetic mini-corpus doc)
Speculative decoding accelerates autoregressive inference by verifying
several drafted tokens in one forward pass of the target model. The speedup
depends on the acceptance rate of the draft, which in turn depends on how
closely the draft distribution tracks the target distribution. We derive
the expected speedup as a function of the acceptance rate and the relative
cost of the draft model, and we show that the optimal draft length is short
even when the acceptance rate is high, because each rejected token wastes a
full target-model evaluation. Experiments on summarization and translation
benchmarks confirm the predicted optimum within measurement noise, and a
distilled draft model raises the acceptance rate enough to shift the
optimum by one token. The analysis explains reported speedups across the
literature and predicts when speculative decoding does not pay.
