# Paper B: adapters for frozen language models (synthetic mini-corpus doc)
Adapters update a small fraction of parameters while the base model stays
frozen, which makes them attractive for deployments that host many tasks on
one checkpoint. We insert low-rank matrices into the attention projections
and show that the rank controls a trade-off between downstream accuracy and
adapter latency. Across a battery of classification and generation tasks,
ranks between eight and sixty-four recover most of the fine-tuning gap,
while ranks above that plateau in accuracy and cost more latency than the
gap justifies. We analyze the optimization geometry of the adapter layer
and show that the frozen backbone keeps the effective rank of the gradient
low, which explains why small ranks suffice. The recipe transfers across
model families with no retuning beyond the rank sweep itself, and the
inference overhead is a single extra matrix product per adapted projection.
